Senior Product Manager
InnovateTech Corporation

Job Description:
We are seeking an experienced Senior Product Manager to lead product strategy and execution for our flagship B2B SaaS platform. The ideal candidate will have a strong background in product management, user experience, and data-driven decision making.

Key Responsibilities:
• Define and execute product strategy and roadmap
• Conduct user research and gather customer feedback
• Collaborate with engineering, design, and marketing teams
• Analyze product metrics and user behavior data
• Lead product launches and go-to-market strategies
• Manage product backlog and prioritize features
• Present product updates to stakeholders and executives
• Drive cross-functional initiatives and process improvements

Required Skills:
• 5+ years of product management experience in B2B SaaS
• Strong analytical skills and data-driven approach
• Experience with user research and usability testing
• Proficiency in product management tools (Jira, Confluence, Figma)
• Understanding of agile development methodologies
• Excellent communication and presentation skills
• Experience with A/B testing and experimentation
• Bachelor's degree in Business, Engineering, or related field

Preferred Skills:
• MBA or advanced degree preferred
• Experience in enterprise software or technology platforms
• Knowledge of UX/UI design principles
• Familiarity with SQL and data analysis tools
• Experience with customer success and support teams
• Understanding of software development lifecycle
• Previous experience in startup or high-growth environments

Technical Skills:
• Analytics: Google Analytics, Mixpanel, Amplitude
• Design: Figma, Sketch, Adobe Creative Suite
• Project Management: Jira, Asana, Monday.com
• Data Analysis: SQL, Excel, Tableau, Power BI
• Prototyping: InVision, Marvel, Balsamiq
• CRM: Salesforce, HubSpot, Intercom

Key Metrics:
• User engagement and retention rates
• Feature adoption and usage metrics
• Customer satisfaction scores (NPS, CSAT)
• Revenue growth and conversion rates
• Time to market for new features
• Product-qualified leads (PQLs)
