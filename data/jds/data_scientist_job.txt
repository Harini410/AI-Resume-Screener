Senior Data Scientist
DataDriven Analytics

Job Description:
We are looking for an experienced Senior Data Scientist to join our analytics team and drive data-driven decision making across the organization. The successful candidate will work on challenging problems involving machine learning, statistical modeling, and big data analytics.

Key Responsibilities:
• Develop and deploy machine learning models for predictive analytics
• Conduct statistical analysis and hypothesis testing
• Build data pipelines and automated reporting systems
• Create data visualizations and dashboards for stakeholders
• Collaborate with product and engineering teams to implement data solutions
• Mentor junior data scientists and analysts
• Present findings and recommendations to executive leadership

Required Skills:
• Master's degree in Data Science, Statistics, or related field
• 4+ years of experience in data science and machine learning
• Expert-level proficiency in Python and SQL
• Experience with ML frameworks (TensorFlow, PyTorch, Scikit-learn)
• Strong statistical analysis and modeling skills
• Experience with big data technologies (Spark, Hadoop)
• Proficiency in data visualization tools (Tableau, Power BI)
• Experience with cloud platforms (AWS, GCP, Azure)

Preferred Skills:
• PhD in quantitative field
• Experience with deep learning and neural networks
• Knowledge of MLOps and model deployment
• Experience with A/B testing and experimentation
• Familiarity with time series analysis and forecasting
• Experience in specific domains (finance, healthcare, e-commerce)
• Strong communication and presentation skills

Technical Requirements:
• Programming: Python, R, SQL, Scala
• Machine Learning: Supervised/unsupervised learning, ensemble methods
• Data Processing: Pandas, NumPy, Spark, Kafka
• Visualization: Matplotlib, Seaborn, Plotly, D3.js
• Cloud: AWS SageMaker, Google AI Platform, Azure ML
• Databases: PostgreSQL, MongoDB, Snowflake, BigQuery
