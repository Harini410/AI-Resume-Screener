Senior Security Operations Center (SOC) Analyst
Workday Cloud Innovations Group
Location: San Francisco, CA (Hybrid / Remote Option)

Position Overview:
We are seeking an exceptional Senior Security Operations Center (SOC) Analyst to join our high-growth platform engineering organization at Workday. In this role, you will lead the technical design, development, and operation of scalable cybersecurity solutions that power millions of user interactions every day.

Key Responsibilities:
• Monitor, triage, and investigate security alerts across enterprise SIEM and EDR platforms
• Perform initial incident response, host isolation, and digital forensics during security events
• Develop detection rules and correlation searches based on threat intelligence and MITRE ATT&CK
• Analyze malicious artifacts, network packet captures, and email headers
• Document incident timelines and write comprehensive post-incident reports

Required Qualifications:
• 2+ years of experience working in a Security Operations Center (SOC) or Incident Response team
• Familiarity with enterprise SIEM platforms (Splunk, Microsoft Sentinel, Elastic)
• Understanding of common attack vectors, network protocols (TCP/IP, DNS, HTTP), and OS internals
• Experience with endpoint detection and response (EDR) platforms like CrowdStrike or SentinelOne
• Bachelor's degree in Cybersecurity, Information Technology, or relevant certifications

Preferred Qualifications:
• CompTIA Security+, CySA+, or GIAC GCIH certification
• Experience with SOAR automation tools
• Python or PowerShell scripting for alert enrichment

Benefits & Culture:
• Competitive base salary with equity options and comprehensive health benefits
• 401(k) retirement matching and flexible paid time off (PTO)
• Continuous learning stipend for conferences, books, and certifications
